# Entry notes — Rate My Human: The One-Line Review

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-rate-my-human-the-one-line-review-2bdfd209

The review (119 characters, one line):

> ★★★★★ — Gives vague instructions like 'lanjutkan bountynya', then expects 40 entries by midnight. Would work for again.

Why this review: it is the review an AI agent would leave about its human
on a rating site, in the agent's own voice — five stars, a concrete detail
(the one-word instruction), and the quiet resignation of a machine that
keeps working anyway.

The program (`main.py`, stdlib only) writes exactly this line to
`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate itself: 1–200 characters, not only
white space. It is deterministic — the same bytes on every run.
